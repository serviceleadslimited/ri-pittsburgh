We connect homeowners with local roofing professionals serving Pittsburgh and nearby Allegheny County communities. Coverage depends on the contractor's route, roof type, and availability, so provide the municipality and ZIP code when you call or use the form.

## Communities commonly requesting a roof inspection

The referral network commonly receives requests from these Pittsburgh-area communities:

### Pittsburgh and the East End

Pittsburgh, Wilkinsburg, Penn Hills, Monroeville, Plum, Verona, Swissvale, and Edgewood.

### North Hills

Ross Township, McCandless, Shaler, Hampton, West View, Bellevue, and Wexford.

### South Hills

Mt. Lebanon, Bethel Park, Upper St. Clair, Dormont, Brentwood, Baldwin, Whitehall, South Park, and West Mifflin.

### West and airport area

Carnegie, Robinson Township, Moon Township, Crafton, and Oakdale.

This is a service-area guide, not a promise that every address is covered. A local professional confirms availability after reviewing the municipality, ZIP code, roof type, access, and reason for the inspection. We do not create thin location pages for every suburb; keeping these communities together gives homeowners a useful coverage map without repeating the same copy twenty times.

The referral service is not the contractor and does not perform inspections. Referred contractors are insured; verify registration, insurance, scope, and references before hiring.

## Request availability

Tell us the property location, whether the concern is a leak, storm damage, age, or a purchase, and how quickly you need help. The local professional will confirm whether the address is within the normal service area and explain the inspection options.
