## How to use this directory

Start with the problem you need solved, not just the company name. A leak after rain, storm damage, a ceiling stain, an aging roof, a home purchase, or an insurance question can call for different inspection scope and documentation.

When you call, ask:

1. Is the inspection free, and what will it cover?
2. Will the roof be accessed when safe, and what areas may be limited by height, pitch, or conditions?
3. Will you receive photographs, a written scope, or a formal report?
4. Can the company explain repair options before recommending replacement?
5. What Pennsylvania home-improvement registration and insurance information applies to the work?
6. Is there any obligation to hire the company after the inspection?

For a home sale, purchase, dispute, HOA decision, or formal insurance documentation, ask whether you need a paid independent inspection rather than a contractor-provided condition visit. Our [roof inspection cost guide](/roof-inspection-cost) explains that difference.
