A directory page can be useful without pretending to certify a list of businesses. This page explains how to evaluate a Pittsburgh roofing professional before you authorize an inspection or repair.

## Why there is no business listing here

Pittsburgh Roof Inspection is a referral service, not a paid directory, licensing authority, or review publisher. We intentionally do **not** publish a rotating list of local contractor names and imply that every business is endorsed, insured, licensed, or currently available. Pennsylvania also does not have one statewide roofing license that this site can use as a blanket approval signal; registration, insurance, inspection practice, and local requirements still need to be checked for the actual work.

The directory page exists as a homeowner decision guide and as a durable local resource: it helps a visitor compare the professional we connect them with, rather than turning the page into an unverified lead list. If a business name appears in a referral conversation, verify it directly before hiring.

## Questions worth asking

- What exactly will the inspection cover?
- Which roof areas cannot be accessed safely?
- Will I receive labeled photographs or a written report?
- What findings make repair reasonable or replacement necessary?
- Are you insured, and what Pennsylvania registration or local requirements apply?
- Who performs the work, and who stands behind it?
- Is the estimate valid for a stated period, and what is excluded?
- Is there any obligation to hire after a free inspection?

If the concern involves an insurance claim, ask the contractor to document observations without promising coverage. If the concern is a home purchase or sale, consider whether an independent inspection is more appropriate than a contractor who wants the repair work.
