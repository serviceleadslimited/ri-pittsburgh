*Last updated: August 2026*

Pittsburgh Roof Inspection operates roofinspectionpittsburgh.com as a free referral service. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information you provide

If you call or submit the referral form, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, municipality, ZIP code, property concern, and the details you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, connect you with an independent local roofing professional, prevent spam, and keep basic operational records.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, medical information, or other sensitive information through the form. The referred contractor may collect additional information under its own privacy policy when you communicate directly.

## Sharing

We share the request details needed to make the referral with the relevant local contractor. We may use service providers for hosting, form delivery, analytics, spam prevention, and call tracking. We do not sell the form information as a list.

## Analytics and calls

The site may use Google Analytics and privacy-respecting site analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve the pages. Calls may be routed through a tracking number so we can measure whether the referral service is working. Call recording or voicemail features may be enabled by the call-routing provider; do not share sensitive information on a call if you do not want it recorded.

## Retention and choices

We keep referral information only as long as reasonably necessary for the referral, operational records, legal obligations, and security. You may contact us through the Contact page to ask what information we hold about a request or to ask that we stop using it for a referral, subject to records we must retain.

The site may link to contractors or third parties. Their privacy practices are separate from ours. We may update this policy when the site's data practices change.
