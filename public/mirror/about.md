Need a clearer next step for a leak, storm concern, ceiling stain, or aging roof? Pittsburgh Roof Inspection makes it simple to request a **free roof inspection** from one local insured roofing professional.

## Start with a clearer look at the roof

Roof issues are rarely convenient. You may have noticed a ceiling mark after rain, a shingle on the ground after wind, a leak that comes and goes, or a roof that is simply getting old. Before you authorize a repair or replacement, a free inspection gives you a practical starting point: what is visible, what deserves attention, and what questions to ask next.

We connect Pittsburgh-area homeowners with one local roofing professional instead of handing your details to a call list. Tell us what changed, where the property is, and whether water is active or a recent storm is involved. That helps the right conversation start faster.

## What you can expect

- **A free inspection request:** Call or use the form to describe the concern and your Pittsburgh-area location.
- **One local connection:** Your request goes to one insured local roofing professional—not a round of unwanted calls.
- **Straightforward next steps:** Ask what was observed, what is accessible, whether photos or a written scope are available, and whether repair may be reasonable before considering a larger job.
- **No obligation to hire:** An inspection is the chance to understand the situation. Any repair or replacement decision comes afterward and is yours to make.

## Good questions lead to better roofing decisions

Whether the concern is a leak, storm damage, roof age, a home purchase, or a prior repair that did not solve the problem, start with the details that matter. Tell the professional when the issue appears, where you see it inside the home, and what weather or event came before it. Ask what they can see, what they cannot safely access, and what evidence supports the recommended next step.

For a sale, purchase, insurance claim, dispute, or formal documentation, ask whether a contractor-provided roof inspection is the right fit or whether you need a paid independent report. Our [roof inspection cost guide](/roof-inspection-cost) explains the difference so you can choose the scope that fits the decision in front of you.

## Ready for a free roof inspection?

Call or use the form with your neighborhood, roof concern, and the best way to reach you. We will make one local connection so you can get a clearer picture without having to call around first.

## A brief note about Pittsburgh Roof Inspection

Pittsburgh Roof Inspection connects homeowners with an independent local roofing professional; it does not perform the inspection or later roofing work itself. Before hiring, confirm the business identity, applicable Pennsylvania registration, insurance, written scope, and references directly with the professional.
