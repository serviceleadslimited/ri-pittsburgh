Pittsburgh Roof Inspection is a free local referral service. We help homeowners start a conversation with an independent roofing professional when they need to understand a leak, storm concern, aging roof, or inspection question.

We are **not the contractor**. We do not climb roofs, diagnose properties, write inspection reports, perform repairs, or decide insurance claims. The contractor we refer is insured, but you should confirm the contractor's identity, applicable Pennsylvania registration, insurance, written scope, and references before hiring.

Our goal is simple: make it easier to request a free roof inspection without pretending that a referral is a guarantee. Ask what the inspection covers, what you receive afterward, whether a repair is reasonable, and whether there is any obligation to authorize work.

Use the form or call to describe the property and the concern. We will pass the request to a local roofing professional serving Pittsburgh and nearby communities.
