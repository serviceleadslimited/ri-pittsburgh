A directory is useful only when the information is current and the comparison criteria are clear. Before hiring any Pittsburgh roofing professional, verify the business name, phone number, service area, insurance, applicable Pennsylvania registration, local requirements, references, and written scope.

Pittsburgh Roof Inspection is a referral service, not a contractor directory that certifies businesses. Listings, referrals, or recommendations do not mean a contractor is licensed, endorsed, or guaranteed by this site.

## Questions worth asking

- What exactly will the inspection cover?
- Which roof areas cannot be accessed safely?
- Will I receive labeled photographs or a written report?
- What findings make repair reasonable or replacement necessary?
- Are you insured, and what registration or local requirements apply?
- Who performs the work, and who stands behind it?
- Is the estimate valid for a stated period, and what is excluded?

If the concern involves an insurance claim, ask the contractor to document observations without promising coverage. If the concern is a home purchase or sale, consider whether an independent inspection is more appropriate than a contractor who wants the repair work.
